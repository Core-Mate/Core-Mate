# Core-Mate

## The AI workforce for social media and growth teams

Core-Mate helps your team run social media outreach, follow-ups and content publishing across accounts. Describe your goal, review the work and let AI agents carry out daily tasks on managed cloud phones.

Your team stays in control of what goes out.

[Visit the website](https://www.core-mate.com/) · [中文介绍](README.zh-CN.md)

## What you can do with Core-Mate

### Reach creators and customers

Coordinate personalized outreach to creators, partners and customers. Set the audience, message and follow-up schedule so your team can manage conversations across accounts.

### Publish and keep conversations moving

Prepare content for review, schedule approved posts and handle replies and comments as part of your daily social media workflow.

### Coordinate work across accounts

Bring tasks for different brands, markets and accounts into one workspace. Run work across multiple cloud phones and follow progress in one place.

### Research your market

Gather information about creators, competitors and relevant content to support campaign planning. Review findings and use them to shape your next actions.

### Review progress and results

Follow task status, inspect execution records and review outputs. Keep publishing decisions and account activity visible to the people responsible for them.

## From a goal to daily execution

1. **Describe the goal.** Tell Core-Mate which accounts, audiences and outcomes matter, or start with a built-in playbook.
2. **Review the work.** Check the proposed steps, content and schedule with your team.
3. **Run the tasks.** Assign work to managed cloud phones and follow progress across accounts.
4. **Review the results.** Check outputs and execution records, then refine the next run.

For example, a product launch can bring together a posting plan for TikTok and X, approved content, scheduled publishing and follow-up on new comments.

## Social apps and cloud phones

Core-Mate supports social media workflows on platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn and X. Available workflows depend on the app, account permissions and your workspace.

Managed cloud phones let your team get started without maintaining its own phone fleet. Teams can also connect their own phones and discuss capacity options as their work grows.
