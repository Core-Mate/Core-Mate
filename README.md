## 💬 The work nobody talks about

A large part of social media outreach remains difficult to hand over to software. Many essential in-app actions lack suitable public integrations.

Think about how your team reaches creators and customers today. Someone opens a prospect spreadsheet. Someone writes a first message that sounds personal. Then come the copy-and-paste, the day-three and day-seven follow-ups, the checks for replies and the status columns that never quite match.

Agencies hire virtual assistants to send messages and update spreadsheets by hand. It works until a list grows from 50 creators to 200, or a client asks for 500 contacts a day. The spreadsheet quietly becomes a bottleneck.

TikTok, Instagram and LinkedIn each place limits on messaging integrations and access. Scheduling tools and social media suites cover publishing and reporting, while some in-app conversations and follow-ups still require manual work.

**That is the gap CoreMate is built to fill.**

## ✨ What is CoreMate?

CoreMate is an AI workforce for social apps. Its agents tap, type, read screens, send messages and record results inside real mobile apps, running on cloud phones we host, update and maintain for you.

Describe a goal in plain language. Agents turn it into steps your team can review, then run tasks in parallel across your configured cloud phones. Execution logs let your team follow the work.

### 📱 Work inside the apps

Handle messaging, in-app follow-ups and platform-specific workflows through the app interface. Available tasks depend on the platform, account permissions and supported workflows.

### 🎯 Built for social media teams

No coding required. Start with a built-in playbook or describe your own task. Agree on steps, content and scope with your team before a run.

### 🤝 Your team stays in control

Your team reviews communication and publishing decisions, sets an appropriate pace for each account and checks results in the logs. People take over when a conversation or platform verification needs attention.

## 🚀 A four-step rollout

### 1. Define your goal

Choose the apps, accounts and workflows, along with the cloud phone capacity you need. Agree on scope and success criteria before running.

### 2. Set up your cloud phones

We prepare your workspace and cloud phones. Sign in to your own accounts inside the phones and get ready for your selected tasks.

### 3. Validate a real workflow

Complete one task end to end. Review logs and outputs, refine the workflow and expand once it works for your team.

### 4. Scale with support

Add accounts and phones as work grows, with operating guides, maintenance as apps change and support from our team.
